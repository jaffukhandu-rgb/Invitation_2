The Beginning

Our Story

Two Families, One Promise

Two families, two journeys, one quiet certainty. What started as a simple introduction became a promise we're now ready to keep, surrounded by the people we love most.

I.

The Beginning

Our families came together first, and in that first conversation was a quiet feeling that this was meant to be. What began as an introduction soon became something neither of us wanted to let go of.

II.

The Nikah

Surrounded by close family and with hearts full of gratitude, our nikah was solemnized on the 10th of October, 2025 in Surat, on dast mubaraka of Shehzada Ammar Bhaisahab Jamaluddin — the day we officially became each other's.

III.

Forever

From the mehndi to the mama mosala, and finally our procession and bidayi at Ammar Nagar, Indore, we bring our families and friends together to celebrate — and we would be honoured to have you share in the joy with us.

"Of His signs is that He created for you mates... and He placed between you affection and mercy."
